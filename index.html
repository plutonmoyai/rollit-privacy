Privacy Policy for Roll It

Roll It (also known as DEVİR) is developed and published by Deadsector. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Roll It is a completely offline, ad-free puzzle game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Location Data: The game does not track or access your geographic location.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, tracking cookies, or device fingerprinting.
  • No Financial Data: The game is 100% free with no in-app purchases or payments.

2. Device Storage and Local Data

Any progress you make in the game (such as campaign level completion, high scores, streaks, and user preferences like sound volume or color-blind mode) is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application.

3. Device Permissions

The game may request permission for Vibration (Haptics). This permission is solely used to provide tactile feedback during gameplay (e.g. block explosions, mine impacts) and does not access any user data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game is suitable for all ages and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not collect data from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
